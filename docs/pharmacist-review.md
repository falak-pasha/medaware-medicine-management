## How to review

For each medicine: read the summary and every section in the app (or in `js/data/medicines.js`), check it against current guidance, and resolve the notes below. Then tell the developer one of:
- **Approved**: the entry's `review` becomes `"reviewed"` and the app's label changes to "Reviewed".
- **Changes needed**: list the corrections (with a source), or ask for the entry to be hidden.

Priorities: the **Books differ** notes first (the textbooks disagree), then notes about guidance that changed after the books were published.
